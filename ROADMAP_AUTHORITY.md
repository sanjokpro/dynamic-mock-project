# Strategic Authority

This repository uses the following documents as the governing product strategy.

Primary Documents:

- AI_GOVERNANCE.md
- MASTER_PRODUCT_ACTION_PLAN.md
- MASTER_PRODUCT_ACTION_PLAN_REVIEW.md

Rules:

1. Product priorities must follow these documents.
2. New feature requests do not automatically become priorities.
3. Any roadmap override requires written business justification.
4. Differentiation takes priority over commodity features.
5. ISO8583, Stateful Scenarios, White Labeling, and Self Hosting are strategic priorities.
6. Auth, RBAC, LDAP, SAML, OIDC, and Multi-Tenancy remain deferred unless roadmap documents are formally updated.

Execution begins after roadmap approval.
